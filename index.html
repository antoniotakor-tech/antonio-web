How it works

Seven simple steps. Zero tech on your side.

  1. You message me on WhatsApp

    Tell me what you do and what you need.

  2. A short call

    15 to 30 minutes about your business and what your customers ask most.

  3. A written quote

    You get the price, the timeline and what’s included in writing. If it works for you, we start.

  4. 50% to start

    You pay half and send me your logo, photos, prices, hours and text. Not sure what to write? I send you a simple checklist.

  5. A private preview

    You get a private link to see your site, usually within 10 working days of receiving everything.

  6. One round of changes

    You tell me what to tweak and I do it before launch.

  7. Online

    You pay the second half and your site goes live.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You send me what I ask for and I do the rest.

What if I don’t have photos or text ready?

We start once you have them. I send you a checklist of what I need. Writing texts and shooting photos are extras.

Can customers book through the site?

They tap the button and message you on WhatsApp. Then you decide when to confirm.

Can the site be in Dutch?

Yes. You send me the Dutch text and I put it in. A second language costs extra.

Who owns the site?

You do, after the final payment.

Will I be first on Google?

I set the site up so Google understands what you do and where. Rankings depend on your area and competition.

How long does it take?

Usually 10 working days from when you’ve sent me everything.

And after it’s online?

If something doesn’t work because of me, I fix it. If you want to change or add something, just message me.
